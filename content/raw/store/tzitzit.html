The Torah commands us in Numbers 15 to make "Tzitzit", often translated "fringes", on the corners of our garments. YHWH tells us that the purpose of these tzitzit, which contain a cord of blue, is that when we see them we, "remember and do all My commandments, and thereby be holy to YHWH your God" (Nu 15:40).
