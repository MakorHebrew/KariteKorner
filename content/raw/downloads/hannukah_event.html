Karaite Hannukah Happening




Join the Jerusalem Karaite Movement for an exciting evening of food, live music, and a talk
by Hacham Nehemia Gordon on the topic:

"Hannukah: Have the Jewish People Been Celebrating a Pagan Holiday?"

Where: Ha-Birah Hotel, Chavatselet 4 (100m from Kikar Zion)
When: Thursday, December 5, (7th Candle of Hannukah), 8:00 PM.
Admission: 10 NIS.

The Karaite Movement is dedicated to returning to pure Torah Judaism as taught in the Tanach.
