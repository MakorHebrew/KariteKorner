WE BEAR WITNESS!

Dear Reader! We bear witness to the following truths, which are essential to YOUR eternal salvation, whether you be Christian, Jewish, Muslim or anything else! Our source of truth is the true and holy Scripture which was delivered unto Israel by Moses and the Prophets. All religions claim to accept this source as a dependable one. The Jewish People throughout the exilic period suffered and died for the sake of this Scripture. We take their word for it that the old Israelite Scripture was WORTH suffering and dying for. We would like to pass the truths of this Scripture along to you.

We bear witness that YEHOVAH IS the only Creator, Saviour and King of the universe; "Oh, YHVH of HOSTS, ELOHEY ISRAEL, that dwelleth upon the Cherubim. Thou art Mighty, even THOU ALONE, of all the kingdoms of the earth; Thou hast made heaven and earth!" Isaiah 37:16. "I, even I am YHVH; and beside ME there is NO SAVIOUR!" Isaiah 43:11. "Before Me there was no god formed, neither shall any be after Me!" Isaiah 43:10. "Whom have I in heaven but THEE? And beside THEE I desire NONE upon earth." Psalms 73:25. "Blessed be Thou YHVH, the Mighty One of Israel, our Father for ever and ever, THINE oh YHVH is the greatness and the power and the glory and the victory and the majesty; for ALL that is in heaven and earth is THINE; THINE is the kingdom oh YHVH, and THOU art exalted as head of ALL!" First Chronicles 29:10-11.

YHVH is named the Mighty One (Eloheem) of Israel, but He is the Father and Saviour of ALL MEN regardless of race or nationality. "Neither let the alien that hath joined himself to YHVH speak, saying: YHVH will surely separate me from His people..... also the aliens that join themselves to YHVH to minister unto Him, and to love the NAME of YHVH to be His servants, every one that keepeth the Sabbath from profaning it, and holdeth fast by My Covenant: even them will I bring to my holy Mountain and make them joyful in My house of prayer!" Isaiah 56:3-8. "Moreover concerning the stranger that is NOT of Thy people Israel, when he shall come out of a far country for Thy GREAT NAME'S SAKE, and Thy mighty hand and Thine outstretched arm; when they shall come and pray toward this house; then hear Thou from heaven, even from Thy dwelling place and do according to all that the stranger calleth to Thee for; that ALL THE PEOPLES of the EARTH MAY KNOW THY NAME and fear Thee as doth Thy people Israel, and that they may know that Thy Name is called upon this house which I have built." Second Chronicles 6:32-33.

This, then is what YHVH requires of you! "It hath been told thee, oh man, what is good, and what YHVH doth require of thee: ONLY to do justly and to love mercy and to walk humbly with thy Almighty One!" Micah 6:8. "Wash you, make you clean, put away the evil of your doings from before mine eyes, cease to do evil; learn to do well; seek justice, relieve the oppressed, judge the fatherless, plead for the widow!" Isaiah 1:16-17.

Dear reader, YHVH will forgive your sins, if you repent! "And when the wicked turneth from his wickedness and doeth that which is lawful and right, he shall live thereby!" Ezekiel 33:19. "He shall surely live, he shall not die! None of his sins that he hath committed shall be remembered against him!" Ezekiel 33:14-16. Look Unto YHVH and be saved! "Look unto Me and be saved, all the end of the earth!" Isaiah 45:22. YHVH is your only victory and strength! "Only in YHVH, shall one say of me, is victory and strength!" Isaiah 45:24. YHVH is your ONLY SAVIOUR! "I even I am YHVH, and beside Me there is NO SAVIOUR!" Isaiah 43:11. YHVH never gives of His Glory to another! "I am YHVH, that is My Name! And My Glory will I NOT give to another!" Isaiah 42:8. YHVH is INCOMPARABLE! "To whom will ye liken Me and make me equal and compare Me, that we may be like?" Isaiah 46:5.

Why is the world in such a mess today, dear reader? YHVH gives the answer. "The earth fainteth and fadeth away, the WORLD FAILETH and fadeth away, the lofty people of the earth do fail. The earth also is defiled under the inhabitants thereof; BECAUSE THEY HAVE TRANSGRESSED THE LAWS, VIOLATED THE STATUTE, BROKEN THE EVERLASTING COVENANT. THEREFORE HATH A CURSE DEVOURED THE EARTH. AND THEY THAT DWELL THEREIN ARE FOUND GUILTY!" Isaiah 24:4-6. "TERROR and THE PIT and THE TRAP ARE UPON THEE, OH INHABITANTS OF THE EARTH!" Isaiah 24:17. Which laws and statutes have been transgressed and broken? The Laws and Statutes of YHVH delivered unto the Fathers, unto Moses and the Prophets. There never was any other Law from YHVH. "Remember ye the Law of Moses My servant which I commanded him in Horeb for all Israel".

But what about the gentile? "There is the same Law holding for the native born and the stranger" Exodus 12:49. YHVH's Commandments are for ALL MEN! ALL MEN WISHING TO GAIN ETERNAL SALVATION must reverence HIS NAME, KEEP HIS SABBATH, OBEY HIM. THUS gentile no less than Jew, is blessed by doing and keeping these things. YHVH SAYS SO IN HIS WORD! We bear witness that it is so! TAKE HEED AND LIVE!
