A BIBLE QUIZ
Questions & Answers about matters vital to you!!

Q: Who is the Creator?
A: "For thus saith YHVH (written YEHOVAH in the Massoretic text; modern scholars prefer YAHVEH or YAHWEH) that created the heavens. He is ELOHEEM (Almighty); that formed the earth & made it ..." ISAIAH 45, 18.

Q: Was YHVH the sole creator?
A: "Who hath meted out the spirit of YHVH or who was His counselor that he might instruct Him?" ISAIAH 40,13.

Q: To whom are we to ascribe greatness?
A: "For I will proclaim the name of YHVH; ascribe greatness unto our Eloheem." DEUT.32,3.

Q: Will He give His glory to another?
A: "I am YHVH, that is my name; and my glory will I NOT give to another." ISAIAH 42,8.

Q: Who is the SAVIOUR?
A: "For I am YHVH thy ELOHEEM, the Holy One of Israel, thy SAVIOUR." ISAIAH 43,3.

Q: Have we need of any saviour beside YHVH?
A: "I even I am YHVH; and beside me there is NO SAVIOUR!!" ISAIAH 43,11.

Q: Which name offers deliverance?
A: "Whosoever shall call on the name of YHVH shall be delivered." JOEL 3,5.

Q: Unto whom must we look for salvation?
A: "Look unto Me (YHVH) and be ye saved, all the ends of the earth." ISAIAH 45,22.

Q: Can only blood atone for sin?
A: By mercy and truth is sin atoned for." PROVERBS 16,6.

Q: Does YHVH prefer something else to meat offerings?
A: "For thou delightest not in sacrifice else would I give it. Thou hast no pleasure in burnt offering. The sacrifices of ELOHEEM are a broken spirit; a broken and contrite heart, oh Eloheem, Thou will not despise." PSALMS 51,18-19.

Q: How can we come unto YHVH?
A: "Take with you words and return unto YHVH. Say unto HIM: 'Forgive all iniquity and accept goodness; thus we shall render instead of bullocks the offering of our lips." HOSEA 14,3.

Q: For whose sake were our sins blotted out?
A: "I (YHVH) even I am He that blotteth out thy transgressions for Mine OWN sake." ISAIAH 43,25.

Q: How can the wicked gain life; by believing in some creed or by practicing righteousness?
A: "Let the wicked forsake his way and the sinner his thoughts and let him return unto YHVH and He will have compassion upon him." ISAIAH 55,7.
"Again when I say to the wicked: thou shalt surely die; If he turn from his sin and do that which is lawful & right .... he shall surely live, he shall not die none of his sins that he hath committed shall be remembered against him etc." EZEKIEL 33,14-19.

Q: Do we need an intercessor or intermediary to approach YHVH?
A: YHVH is near to all that call upon Him, to all that call upon Him in truth." PSALMS 145,18

Dear reader, scripture speaks for itself. All the churches, creeds, sects, and theories cannot change fact, Biblical fact. This tract is a testimony to the truth of plain, unadorned Scripture.

Do your present beliefs clash with the above-presented facts? If they do, you have some serious thinking to do.

YHVH the only Saviour is calling you today. Won't you come to Him now?
