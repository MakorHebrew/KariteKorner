THE SON OF DAVID

HOW TO IDENTIFY HIM!


"And I YHVH will be their Almighty, and My Servant David prince among them." Ezekiel 34:24

"And a shoot shall come out of the stock of Jesse, and a twig shall grow out of his roots. And the spirit of YHVH shall rest ON him, the spirit of wisdom and understanding, the spirit of counsel and might, the spirit of knowledge and the fear of YHVH. And he shall delight in the fear of YHVH; and he will not pass judgement on the basis of mere appearances nor give verdict on the basis of rumour; but will judge the poor justly and decide with equity for the poor of the land; and he shall smite the land with the rod of his mouth, and with the breath of his lips shall he slay the wicked (NOT be slain by the wicked himself!). And justice shall be the girdle of his loins, and faithfulness the belt around his waist. And the wolf shall sit with the lamb, and the leopard shall lie down next to the kid; and the calf and lion's cub and the fatling shall be together, and a little child shall lead them. And the cow and the bear shall eat together; their young shall lie down together; and the lion shall eat straw like the ox. And an infant shall play over a serpent's hole and a young child shall put his hand into a viper's den. No one shall hurt or destroy in all My holy mountain; for the earth shall be full of the knowledge of YHVH, just as the waters fill the sea! And he will assemble the dispersed of Israel, and gather together the scattered of Judah from the four corners of the earth!" Isaiah 11:1-16.

WHEN A MAN COMES WHO WILL ACCOMPLISH ALL THIS, THERE SHALL BE NO DOUBT THAT HE IS THE PROMISED DAVIDIC RULER! THERE WILL BE NO NEED FOR MISSIONARIES, CRUSADES, REVIVALS, ETC.!! ALL THE PROPAGANDA ON EARTH CAN'T CHANGE A COUNTERFEIT INTO THE REAL THING, NOR A FRAUD INTO SOMETHING GENUINE.
