SALVATION
In the Light of the Hebrew Bible

The Saviour

"I, even I, am YHVH! and beside Me there is no saviour." -Isaiah 43:11

"Israel is saved in YHVH with an everlasting salvation." -Isaiah 45:17

"Look unto Me (saith YHVH) and be ye saved, all the ends of the earth." -Isaiah 45:22

"Thou shall have no other gods before Me!" -Exodus 20:3

How to be Saved

"He that concealeth his transgressions shall not succeed; but whoso confesseth and forsaketh them shall obtain mercy." -Proverbs 28:13

"Again when I say to the wicked: thou shalt surely die; if he turn from his sin and do that which is lawful and right; if the wicked restore the pledge, return that obtained by robbery, walk in the STATUTES OF LIFE, committing no iniquity; he shall surely live, he shall not die. None of the sins he committed will be remembered against him.... And when the wicked turneth from his wickedness and doeth what is lawful and right, HE SHALL LIVE THEREBY." -Ezekiel 33:14-19

RIGHTEOUSNESS

"Wash you, make you clean, put away the evil of your doings from before Mine eyes, CEASE TO DO EVIL; learn to DO WELL; seek justice, relieve the oppressed, judge the fatherless, plead for the widow." -Isaiah 1:16-17

"To loose the fetters of wickedness, to undo the bands of the yoke, and to let the oppressed go free, and that ye break every yoke... is it not to deal thy bread to the hungry, and that thou bring the poor that are cast out, to thy house... When thou seest the naked, that thou cover him, and that thou hide not thyself from thine own flesh... and thy righteousness shall go before thee, the glory of YHVH shall be thy protection from behind. Then shalt thou call and YHVH will answer; thou shalt cry, and He will say: Here am I." -Isaiah 58:6-9

"It hath been told thee, oh man, what is good, and what YHVH demandeth of thee, ONLY TO ACT JUSTLY, AND TO LOVE MERCY AND TO WALK HUMBLY WITH THINE ALMIGHTY." -Micah 6:8
