New Moon Report
May 2002
Third Month Part 1

On Monday May 13, 2002, observers accross Israel looked for the moon but it was not sighted. There was a light haze on the horizon. Due to the borderline astronomical conditions of this moon, we will be looking again tomorrow night for the new moon.
