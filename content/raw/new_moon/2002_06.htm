New Moon Report
June 2002
Fourth Month

On Wednesday June 12, 2002, the New Moon was sighted by observers from Jerusalem, Israel. The moon was first seen by Roy Hoffman from northern Jerusalem at 19:37. The moon was then sighted from the same location by T'yoma Michael at 19:38, by Devorah Gordon at 19:41, and by Nehemia Gordon at 19:46. The moon was also sighted by Bruce and Hen-el Brill at 19:58 from Tekoa.
